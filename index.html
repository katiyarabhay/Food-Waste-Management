Mission

About HappiPlates

Welcome to HappiPlates, a community-driven platform dedicated to combating hunger and promoting food security for those in need. Our mission is simple: to connect generous individuals, businesses, and organizations with people who require access to nutritious meals. Every day, millions of tons of food go to waste while many face hunger, and we believe that together, we can make a difference.


We provide an easy and transparent way for donors to contribute food to those who need it most. Through partnerships with local food banks, shelters, and community outreach programs, we ensure that every donation reaches the right hands. Whether it's surplus food from businesses, home-cooked meals, or monetary contributions to purchase fresh produce, we are here to facilitate and organize the process.


At HappiPlates, we believe in the power of community and the impact of small acts of kindness. Together, we can help create a world where no one has to go hungry. Join us in our efforts to feed families, support communities, and build a better, more compassionate future for all.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can donate food through HappiPlates?

+

Anyone can donate! We accept food donations from individual households, local grocery stores, wedding organizers, corporate events, and restaurants. Every contribution counts.

How does the real-time tracking work?

+

Once you submit a donation, a delivery partner accepts the task. Using their delivery dashboard, they share their location during pickup and transit, allowing you to trace the journey of your donation on a Leaflet map in real time.

Is there a registration fee for volunteers?

+

No, there is absolutely no registration fee. Signing up as a donor or a delivery partner is entirely free. Our goal is to reduce hunger and waste collaboratively.
